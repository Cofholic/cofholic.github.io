About

I’m a video and sound editor, AI content creator, and 3D artist with 2.5 years of freelance experience, specializing in visual storytelling across video editing, sound design, and AI-generated content. Alongside that, I design graphics and brand identities. I’m building my craft at MAAC (B.Voc in 3D Animation & VFX, 2025–28).

AI Micro-Drama Editing

Premiere Pro, Adobe Audition, Suno AI, Ultimate Vocal Remover
